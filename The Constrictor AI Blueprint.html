05 · How it works day to day

Two workflows, start to finish

A new member asks about classes

  1. Someone messages BBG on Facebook or calls.Cody or the front desk answers today
  2. They paste the message into Claude: "reply to this inquiry."Front-desk skill
  3. Claude drafts a friendly reply: a free first class (any class: boxing, kickboxing, jiu-jitsu, wrestling or MMA), the classes that fit them, and the Gymdesk signup link. Parents sign the waiver there first.Uses the real schedule & prices
  4. Cody or the front desk sends it and the trial goes on the calendar.Calendar connector
  5. The lead lands in the Leads sheet. After the trial, Claude drafts the thank-you and follow-up for the front desk to send.Leads sheet · front-desk skill
  6. Every Friday, Cody uploads the Gymdesk attendance export and Claude lists members who haven't been in for a month, each with a check-in message ready.Gymdesk export · Cody uploads it

Landing a CCCL sponsor

  1. "Find 25 sponsor prospects around Peterborough for the February show."Web search · sponsor-desk skill
  2. Claude adds them to the Sponsorship Pipeline with a reason each one fits.Drive connector
  3. It drafts a personal pitch for the right package, from Bronze ($1,000) to Title ($7,500). 2026 sponsors get first pick.Skill · Gmail drafts
  4. Cody reads, edits and sends from ontario.cccl@gmail.com.Cody approves
  5. Every Monday, Cody asks "do my follow-ups" and Claude lists who to nudge and drafts each message.Sponsor-desk skill
  6. Signed sponsors get a delivery checklist: banner made, posts done, tickets sent.Contacted → interested → proposal → signed → paid

07 · The weekly rhythm

Scheduled, drafted, approved

Every weekdayCloud scheduled task, so it runs with the laptop shut. Morning report: today's calendar, new inquiries, sponsor follow-ups due, tasks overdue, anything waiting for approval.
MondayThe morning report adds last week's numbers. Cody asks for the sponsor follow-up list; Claude drafts the nudges.
FridayCody uploads the Gymdesk export for the quiet-member list with check-in drafts, and asks for next week's post drafts.
After each showResults report: tickets, sponsors, costs, profit. The Predictor gets updated with the real numbers.
MonthlyOpportunity scan: grants, events, towns, sponsors worth a look.

House rules

  • Claude drafts, people send. Cody approves sponsor deals, money, contracts, fight matchups and anything public about CCCL. Staff approve routine gym replies and class reminders.
  • Kids' details stay in Gymdesk. Claude works from first names and attendance only.
  • No health, medical or card details go into Claude or the shared Sheets.
  • Second Chance stays separate. Its own folder, tracker, email and bank account. Participants are a first name and a code, nobody records what someone was convicted of, and Claude never decides who is eligible.
  • A sponsorship isn't revenue until it's paid.
