# BBG first employee: what must be in place (Ontario)

Researched Oct 20, 2026. Factual research, not legal advice. Dates are each page's last-updated date.

## 1. Health and safety (OHSA)
- **Policies:** every employer needs health and safety, workplace violence and workplace harassment policies, reviewed at least yearly. They must be written and posted only at **6+ workers**; at 5 or fewer, only if an inspector orders it. [https://www.ontario.ca/page/health-and-safety-small-businesses, Oct 7, 2026]
- **Harassment program:** must be **written at any size**. It covers reporting (including to someone else if the employer is the alleged harasser), investigation, confidentiality, and sharing results in writing with both parties. Review it yearly. [https://ontario.ca/document/guide-occupational-health-and-safety-act/part-iii0i-workplace-violence-and-workplace-harassment, Aug 17, 2026]
- **Violence:** assess the risk; the program covers controlling risks, summoning help, reporting and investigating. Instruct workers on both programs. [same]
- **Posters (any size):** "Health & Safety at Work: Prevention Starts Here" (English and the majority language) plus a copy of the OHSA, on the wall or electronically. [https://www.ontario.ca/page/posters-required-workplace, Jul 21, 2025]
- **Training:** every worker and supervisor needs basic awareness training, and the employer keeps records. The Ministry's eLearning is free, takes 45–60 minutes and gives a certificate. [https://www.ontario.ca/page/worker-health-and-safety-awareness-four-steps, Dec 4, 2025] [https://www.ontario.ca/page/supervisor-health-and-safety-awareness-five-steps, Dec 4, 2025]
- **First aid (Reg. 1101):** applies to WSIB-covered businesses.
  - **1–5 workers per shift:** a box stocked to the s.8 list (St. John Ambulance manual, dressings, gauze, bandages), in the charge of a worker with Basic (formerly Emergency) First Aid.
  - **6–15 workers:** Intermediate (formerly Standard) First Aid.
  - **All sizes:** post Form 82, inspect the box quarterly and log every treatment.

  [https://www.wsib.ca/en/firstaid, Jul 22, 2026] [https://www.wsib.ca/sites/default/files/2025-03/0445A_FirstAidReqs202502.pdf, form 07/2026]
- **Minimum ages:** 14 for offices, stores and arenas; 15 for factories and restaurant kitchens; no one under 16 during school hours. [https://www.ontario.ca/page/minimum-age-work, Feb 15, 2022]

## 2. Employment standards (ESA)
- **ESA poster:** give each employee a copy within **30 days of hire**; it no longer has to be posted. [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/mandatory-information-employees, Nov 18, 2025]
- **Written hire information** (name, contact, wage, pay period, hours): in force **July 1, 2025** (not 2022/23), only for **25+ employees**, with no 7-day rule. Optional for BBG. [same]
- **Minimum wage:** $17.95, or $16.90 for students **under 18** working in the summer or on school breaks, from Oct 1, 2026 to Sep 30, 2027. [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/minimum-wage, Sep 28, 2026]
- **Vacation pay:** 4%. It can go on each cheque if the employee agrees in writing. [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/vacation, Feb 5, 2026]
- **Public holidays:** **Canada Day** is the only one in July–August. The Civic Holiday is **not** a public holiday. Holiday pay = the previous 4 weeks' wages plus vacation pay, ÷ 20. [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/public-holidays, Jul 8, 2024]
- **Hours:** an unpaid 30-minute eating period after at most 5 hours. The limits are 8 h a day and 48 h a week. [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/hours-work, Jul 8, 2024]
- **Records:** name, address, start date, hours, wages and vacation. Keep **3 years** (vacation 5). [https://www.ontario.ca/document/your-guide-employment-standards-act-0/record-keeping, Feb 5, 2026]

## 3. WSIB
- **Registration:** register within **10 calendar days** of the first hire if coverage is mandatory. Coverage "isn't mandatory for every business." Phone 1-800-387-0750. [https://www.wsib.ca/en/howtoregister, Jul 30, 2026]
- **Mandatory industries:** set by O. Reg. 175/98; Schedule 1 Part II industries are not mandatory. [https://www.wsib.ca/en/operational-policy-manual/schedules-1-2, Dec 5, 2024]
- **Gyms, from secondary sources:**
  - The Office of the Employer Adviser lists "personal physical fitness training services" in Part II. [https://www.employeradviser.ca/non-construction/coverage-2/, Apr 30, 2021]
  - A Brock University report says gyms are outside compulsory coverage. [https://brocku.ca/social-sciences/labour-studies/wp-content/uploads/primary-site/sites/147/Work-in-Ontario-Gyms-and-Fitness-Clubs-Report-FINAL-v1.pdf, May 2019]
  - The Toronto Star reported that 24 of 1,653 fitness businesses had opted in. [https://www.pressreader.com/canada/toronto-star/20201019/281565178244172, Oct 2020]
- **Optional coverage:** apply online and sign the Employer by Application form. Rights and obligations match mandatory coverage. [https://www.wsib.ca/en/application-coverage-businesses, Sep 22, 2025]
- **Premiums:** insurable earnings × rate ÷ 100. [https://www.wsib.ca/en/businesses/premiums-and-payment/calculating-your-premiums, Jul 13, 2026] The 2026 average rate is $1.23; Class O Leisure & Hospitality is $0.90. [https://www.wsib.ca/en/2026premiumrates, Jul 13, 2026] My estimate: 280 h × $17.95 ≈ $5,026, so about $45 at $0.90.
- **CSJ:** youth "must be covered by Workers' Compensation or equivalent liability insurance at the start of their employment." The cost is reimbursed for not-for-profits only. [https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/esdc-edsc/documents/services/funding/canada-summer-jobs/canada-summer-jobs-applicant-guide.pdf, 2026 edition]

## 4. CRA payroll
- **Payroll (RP) account:** register before the first remittance is due, which is the 15th of the month after the first deductions. Withhold income tax, CPP and EI. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/open-manage-payroll-account/determine-need-register.html, Feb 27, 2026]
- **Remitting:** new employers remit **monthly** unless told otherwise. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/open-manage-payroll-account/after-you-register.html, Sep 5, 2025] Quarterly remitting needs monthly withholding under $1,000 and perfect compliance. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/remitting-source-deductions/how-when-remit-more-information.html, May 28, 2025]
- **Before the first pay:** get the SIN and TD1 federal and Ontario forms. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/set-up-new-employee.html, Jan 2, 2026]
- **CPP:** only for ages **18–69**; the employer matches it. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/canada-pension-plan-cpp.html, Jul 15, 2026]
- **EI:** no age limit; the employer pays 1.4×. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/payroll-deductions-contributions/employment-insurance-ei.html, Nov 15, 2022]
- **T4:** due the last day of February, so Feb 29, 2028 for 2027 pay. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/businesses/topics/payroll/file-information-returns-slip-summaries/when-to-file.html, Jul 3, 2025]
- **Calculator:** the Payroll Deductions Online Calculator (PDOC) works out CPP, EI and tax. [https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/e-services/digital-services-businesses/payroll-deductions-online-calculator.html, Jun 12, 2026]

## 5. Working with children
- **Background checks:** no Ontario law requires them for camps. On Aug 19, 2026 the Premier's office pledged to make them mandatory; I found no bill. Ontario Camps Association members must do vulnerable sector checks. [https://www.hrreporter.com/focus-areas/recruitment-and-staffing/ford-pledges-to-make-background-checks-mandatory-for-kids-camps-in-ontario/394792, Aug 2026]
- **Child care licensing:** the Child Care and Early Years Act does not apply to camps for children **4 and older** or to skill-based recreation programs. [https://www.ontario.ca/page/child-care-rules-child-care-and-early-years-act, Oct 28, 2025]
- **CSJ (2026):** no vulnerable-persons screening rule. It does require health and safety practices (WHMIS, hazard list, training, emergency plan), anti-harassment measures, supervision and mentoring plans, and written notice of any injury.

  [https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/funding/canada-summer-jobs/applicant-guide/assess-application.html, Apr 7, 2026] [https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/funding/canada-summer-jobs/applicant-guide/agreement-2025.html, Apr 7, 2026]

## First-hire checklist for BBG
1. **WSIB:** confirm the classification. If mandatory, register within 10 days of hiring. If not, get optional WSIB or equivalent insurance before day 1 (CSJ requires it).
2. **CRA:** open the RP payroll account; the CSJ form asks for it (see `csj2027.md`).
3. **Written documents:** the harassment program (required), the three policies and a violence risk assessment. Review yearly.
4. **Posters:** health and safety poster, OHSA copy, and Form 82 if covered by WSIB.
5. **Training:** Worker 4 Steps for the youth and Supervisor 5 Steps for the supervisor. File the certificates.
6. **First aid:** stock a Reg. 1101 kit, keep one Basic First Aid holder on site, and keep an inspection card and treatment log.
7. **Pay:** $17.95/h (student rate only if under 18), 4% vacation pay, Canada Day pay, 30-minute meal breaks.
8. **ESA poster:** hand it over within 30 days. A written offer is optional.
9. **Each pay:** collect the SIN and TD1 forms first, run PDOC, remit by the 15th, and issue the T4 by Feb 29, 2028.
10. **Records:** keep ESA records 3 years (vacation 5) and CSJ records 6 years after the agreement (s.21.2).
11. **Screening:** make vulnerable sector checks BBG policy and watch for the camp law.
