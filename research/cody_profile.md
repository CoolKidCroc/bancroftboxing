Compiled Oct 20, 2026. Sherdog, Bancroft This Week (BTW) and The Highlander all tie "The Constrictor" to Bancroft, so they describe the same person.

### Boxing
- **Feb 9, 2019:** BTW previewed a 4-round pro bout against Jordan McCue (*Homecoming IV*, Peterborough Memorial Centre), his first fight in Ontario [BTW 2019]. No result was found, and BoxRec is behind a login wall.

### Background
- **NHHS:** wrestling gold medals, then helped coach the team [BTW 2019]. Also played T-Hawks football [https://bancroft-boxing-grappling.gymdesk.com/instructors/bio/id/44537].
- **Wrestling titles:** COSSA titles, and competed at nationals with the Kawartha Olympic Wrestling Club [Examiner 2017].

## 2. News coverage
- **Examiner, "In school of Hard Knocks" (Feb 10, 2017):** "I told them I didn't come here to do nothing. I came here to fight." His goal was the top of the UFC.
- **BTW, "Purtell's first fight on home soil" (Jan 8, 2019):** He was teaching boxing at LiveFit Bancroft. Quote: "if you have a dream, fight for it! No matter how many times you get knocked down. Get back up and keep going."
- **BTW, "Bancroft Boxing expanding programs for all ages" (Feb 18, 2026):** [https://www.bancroftthisweek.com/15539-2/]
  - Opened April 2025, with about 12 members that summer and about 80 by February 2026.
  - Added kids' classes, women's boxing, yoga and family jiu-jitsu.
  - Quote: "We're not just running a gym here; we're building a platform for local talent."
- **Highlander, "Fight night coming to Haliburton County" (Mar 12, 2026):**
  - CCCL debut May 2 at A.J. LaRue Arena: 8–12 amateur bouts, sanctioned by the Ontario Athletic Commission, livestreamed.
  - Projected 800–1,000 spectators and up to $80K for the local economy.
  - Gym had about 90 members.
  - Quote: "I want this to be something that people will travel to Haliburton for."
- **Sherdog, CCCL 1 (May 2, 2026):** Six bouts are listed. Main event: Jake Parsons def. Ken Lockhart, armbar R1 [https://www.sherdog.com/events/CCCL-1-Cottage-Country-Combat-League-112672].
- **Conflict:** BTW says the gym opened in April 2025. The Highlander says "last summer."
- **Not found:** no coverage from mybancroftnow/Moose FM, the Haliburton Echo or CTV, and no public source for "400 tickets / 15 sponsors."

## 4. Inspiration: Canadian coach brands built on content
- **Jordan Preisinger, Limitless Martial Arts (Trenton, ON):**
  - Head coach of the gym, founded 2015 [https://letsroll.co/gym/limitless-martial-arts-trenton-trenton-on].
  - His "Jordan Teaches Jiujitsu" channel has about 316K YouTube followers. It posts sparring with live commentary, technique tutorials and Patreon Q&As [https://creatordb.app/creatorstats/jordanteachesjiujitsu/].
  - Closest match to Cody: small eastern Ontario city.
- **Firas Zahabi, Tristar Gym (Montreal):** YouTube @CoachZahabi has about 327K followers and covers technique and training philosophy [https://www.heepsy.com/top-youtube/mma/canada].
- **Kajan Johnson (ex-UFC):** YouTube @CoachKajan has about 107K followers. He posts MMA instruction and markets seminars to gym owners [heepsy, same link].
