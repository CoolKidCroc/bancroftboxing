# CSJ 2027: findings for Bancroft Boxing & Grappling

Researched October 20, 2026. Tags in brackets link to the sources at the bottom, which give each page's modified date. Most of the guide still describes **2026**.

## 1. Application window
- **Confirmed:** the 2027 call opens **Oct 27, 2026, 9:00 am EST**. [G0]
- **Close date: not published.** The 2026 call ran Nov 4 to Dec 11, 2025. [G4]
- **Ways to apply (2026):** [G4]
  - GCOS (recommended).
  - Online form, which expires 72 hours after you start it.
  - Paper form EMP5396A, by mail (postmarked by the deadline) or in person at Service Canada.
  - **Email and fax are not accepted.**
- **Ontario mail and phone:** PO Box 538, Stn Don Mills, North York ON M3C 0N9; 1-800-935-5555. [G11]
- **GCOS setup:** bank sign-in or GCKey, then register the BN, then verify identity through CRA My Account or at Service Canada. [G12] Set this up before the deadline. [G4]

## 2. Eligibility
- **Size:** private sector needs "50 or fewer full-time employees" (30+ h a week). [G0][G1] With no full-time staff, enter "0". [G2]
- **Sole proprietorships qualify.** [G1]
- **Registration:** a valid CRA BN is required, and the business must be "solely responsible for the management of CSJ-funded jobs." [G1]
- **RP number:** Q1 asks for the 15-digit payroll (RP) number, and the guide says "you must have an RP business number before hiring youth." [G2] GCOS also says to use the RP number. [G4]
- **No payroll account yet:**
  - CRA requires one "before the first remittance due date." [C1]
  - Business Registration Online is fastest. [C2]
  - **Open it before Oct 27** so Q1 can be answered.
- **Ineligible activities:** [G1]
  - partisan activity
  - fundraising to cover the youth's salary
  - "personal service to the employer"
  - displacing "employees or volunteers" (BBG's coaches are volunteers)

## 3. Funding and job rules
- **Subsidy:** "up to 50%" of minimum wage for private sector. [G0] It is based on the adult rate, and **MERCs (CPP/EI/vacation pay) are not reimbursed**. [G6]
- **Ontario minimum wage:** **$17.95/h** from Oct 1, 2026 to Sept 30, 2027 (student rate $16.90). [O1]
- **Estimate:** about $8.98/h, so about $2,513 for 8 weeks at 35 h, up to about $5,744 for 16 weeks at 40 h. This is my calculation using 2026 rules.
- **Job rules (2026):** [G1]
  - 6–16 consecutive weeks, 30–40 h a week.
  - Under 30 h may be ineligible, so no part-time.
  - Jobs ran Apr 20 to Aug 29, 2026.
  - Youth must be 15–30 at the start, a citizen, PR or protected person, with a valid SIN.
- **Approval length:** ESDC aims for "about 8 weeks" at 35 h and may approve fewer weeks. [G5] Changes need approval first. [G9]

## 4. Application sections (2026 form) [G2]
- **Part A:** RP number, contacts, year established, activities, number of full-time employees, civic address of the work, other wage funding, debts to the Government of Canada, health and safety, Workers' Compensation, anti-harassment.
- **Part B:** title, wage, tasks, skills development, supervision plan, mentoring plan, intent to retain, recruiting youth facing barriers, national and local priorities.
- **Part C:** costs, advance request, attestation.
- **Scoring (out of 100):** [G3]
  - job quality 30 (wage above minimum 10, retention 5, supervision 15)
  - skills 40 (mentoring 13, transferable skills 27)
  - priorities 30 (youth facing barriers, national, local; 10 each)
- **National priorities: 2027 not published.** The 2026 priorities were: [G3]
  - construction
  - green jobs and environment
  - technology and digital skills, including foundational AI skills

## 5. Riding and local priorities
- **Riding:** **Hastings—Lennox and Addington—Tyendinaga**. Its boundaries list "the towns of Bancroft…" [E1] MP: Shelby Kramp-Neuman (Conservative). [E2]
- **Postal code** decides the constituency, and the address must be a civic address. [G2]
- **Local priorities: 2027 not published.** 2026: [G7]
  - not-for-profit organizations
  - cultural events
  - community events
  - cultural community support
  - local and regional tourism development
- **MP input:** MPs "can provide input" on projects. [Q1]

## 6. 2027 changes and timeline
- **Earlier opening:** Oct 27 versus Nov 4 last year. [G0][G4]
- **Fewer jobs:** funding covers "100,000 jobs in Summer 2026 and up to 70,000 jobs in 2027," so expect more competition. [Q1]
- **Proposals only:** the PBO (Oct 15, 2026) costed Senate proposals to double the number and length of jobs. They have not been enacted. [P1]
- **Nothing else** has been published for 2027.
- **2026 timeline:** decisions came by email "starting in April 2026." [G5] Hiring ran Apr 20 to Jul 20. [N1] There is no appeal. [G5]

## 7. Common first-timer mistakes
- **Starting work before written approval.** That time is not reimbursed. [G5]
- **Missing the 5-business-day window** to answer information requests. [G3]
- **Hiring an owner's family member** without prior written approval. [G8]
- **Expecting payment early.** Nothing is paid until the Employer and Employee Declaration and the claim are processed. [G10]
- **Missing WSIB registration,** due within 10 days of the first hire for most businesses. [W1]
