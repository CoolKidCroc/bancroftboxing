# BBG Skills Passport levels

The same as the Levels tab of the BBG Skills Passport sheet. Five skills per level; an athlete moves up when all five are passed.

## Kids Boxing

**Level 1: Rookie**
- Stance and guard: Holds a good stance and guard for 20 seconds while the coach checks (drill: Stance and guard)
- Step-drag footwork: Steps forward, back, left and right without crossing the feet (drill: Step-drag footwork)
- Jab on pads: 10 jabs on the pads, each one back to the guard (drill: Jab on pads)
- Rope-less skipping: 30 seconds of jump-rope footwork (no rope) (drill: Jump rope)
- Gym rules: Stops on the whistle, takes water breaks, puts gloves on and off safely

**Level 2: Contender**
- 1-2 on pads: Jab-cross on the pads with the back foot pivoting (drill: 1-2 on pads)
- Footwork ladder: One ladder pattern start to finish without stopping (drill: Footwork ladder and cones)
- Slips: Slips left and right along the slip line (drill: Slip line)
- Bag round: One 1-minute heavy bag round with the guard up (drill: Heavy bag rounds)
- Glove tag: Stays in stance for a whole round of glove tag (drill: Glove tag)

**Level 3: Challenger**
- Jab-step-jab: Jab, step in, jab on the pads (drill: Jab on pads)
- Slip and counter: Slips, then jabs straight back, on the slip line (drill: Slip line)
- Bob and weave: Ducks under the low rope with the knees, guard up (drill: Bob and weave under the rope)
- Pad target speed: Hits the right pad on the coach's call, quickly and cleanly (drill: Pad target game)
- Two bag rounds: Two 1-minute bag rounds with good stance (drill: Heavy bag rounds)

**Level 4: Champion**
- Combination: 1-2, slip, 1-2 on the pads (drill: 1-2 on pads)
- Pivots: Pivots and changes direction on the coach's call (drill: Follow the leader footwork)
- Three bag rounds: Three 1-minute bag rounds (drill: Heavy bag rounds)
- Helps a newer kid: Shows a newer kid the stance and guard (drill: Stance and guard)
- Leads the warm-up: Leads part of the warm-up for the class (drill: Animal walks)

## Wrestling

**Level 1: Rookie**
- Stance and motion: Moves forward, back and in circles in a good stance (drill: Stance and motion)
- Back breakfall: Back breakfall from squatting: chin tucked, slaps the mat (drill: Breakfalls)
- Side breakfall: Side breakfall on both sides (drill: Breakfalls)
- Sprawl: Sprawls on the whistle and gets back to stance (drill: Sprawl drill)
- Shoulder roll: Forward and back shoulder rolls over the shoulder, not the neck (drill: Grappling movement warm-up)

**Level 2: Contender**
- Penetration step (knees): Penetration step from the knees, back straight, head up (drill: Penetration step)
- Knee tag: Protects the knees in stance for a whole round (drill: Knee tag)
- Sumo push: Stays low and drives with the legs (drill: Sumo push)
- Technical stand-up: Stands up in base with a hand protecting the face (drill: Technical stand-up)
- The tap: Taps clearly and lets go at once when a partner taps (drill: Tap practice)

**Level 3: Challenger**
- Penetration step (standing): Level change and penetration step from standing (drill: Penetration step)
- Half nelson turn: Turns a partner with pressure on the shoulder, never the neck (drill: Half nelson turn)
- Turtle defence: Keeps elbows tight so a partner can't flip them (drill: Turtle flip)
- Hip escape: Shrimps the length of the mat both ways (drill: Shrimp chains)
- Live go: Uses a move from class in live wrestling from the knees (drill: Live wrestling from the knees)

**Level 4: Champion**
- Sprawl and go behind: Sprawls on a shot and comes around behind (drill: Sprawl drill)
- Pin with control: Holds a pin for 3 seconds with control (drill: Half nelson turn)
- Sportsmanship: Shakes hands, follows the coach's calls, wins and loses well
- Teaches breakfalls: Shows a newer kid the back breakfall (drill: Breakfalls)
- Conditioning: 10 sprawls back to stance in 30 seconds (drill: Sprawl drill)

## Boxing 11-14

**Level 1: Rookie**
- Stance and guard: Good stance and guard, weight on the balls of the feet (drill: Stance and guard)
- Footwork: Step-drag in all four directions in stance (drill: Step-drag footwork)
- Jab and 1-2: Jab and 1-2 on the pads with the hands back to the face (drill: 1-2 on pads)
- Rope-less skipping: Two minutes of jump-rope footwork (no rope) (drill: Jump rope)
- Wraps and rules: Wraps own hands correctly and knows the gym's safety rules

**Level 2: Contender**
- 1-2-3: Jab, cross, lead hook on the pads (drill: 1-2-3 on pads)
- Called combinations: Throws the coach's called combinations without dropping the hands (drill: Coach-called combinations)
- Slip and counter: Slips both sides on the line with a counter (drill: Slip line)
- Bag rounds: Two 2-minute bag rounds (drill: Heavy bag rounds)
- Footwork ladder: Ladder patterns at speed, guard up (drill: Footwork ladder and cones)

**Level 3: Challenger**
- Bob and weave: Bob and weave under the rope with the knees (drill: Bob and weave under the rope)
- Body touch control: Body-only touch drill: stays light and in control (drill: Body-only partner touch)
- Step out: Finishes every combination with a step out, hands up (drill: Coach-called combinations)
- Three bag rounds: Three 2-minute bag rounds with good form (drill: Heavy bag rounds)
- Angles: Pivots to an angle after a combination (drill: Footwork ladder and cones)

**Level 4: Champion**
- Leads the warm-up: Leads the class warm-up (drill: Dynamic warm-up)
- Holds pads safely: Holds the pads safely for a partner's jab and 1-2 (drill: Jab on pads)
- Three light rounds: Three body-only touch rounds, staying light (drill: Body-only partner touch)
- Helps newer athletes: Helps a newer athlete with stance and footwork
- Safety leader: Can explain the gym's safety rules, including what to do after a hit to the head
