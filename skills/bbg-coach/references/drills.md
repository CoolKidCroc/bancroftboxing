# BBG drill library

63 drills, the same as the Drills tab of the BBG Coaching Library. Contact levels: None · Pads/bag · Partner drilling · Live grappling · Light touch (body) · Controlled sparring.

## Warm-up

### Dynamic warm-up
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Open floor · 10 min
- How: Jog, high knees, butt kicks, side shuffles, carioca, leg swings, arm circles, inchworms; then balance (stand on one leg, eyes on the coach, 20 sec each side) and 5 squat jumps landing softly.
- Coaching points: Build from easy to quick. Land quietly with knees over toes.
- Safety: Every class starts with this or the grappling warm-up. Water break after.

### Animal walks
- Ages: Kids 4-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats or open floor · 6 min
- How: Lengths of the mat: bear crawl, crab walk, frog jumps, gorilla shuffle, inchworm. Call the animal; kids race the clock, not each other.
- Coaching points: Hands flat, hips low on bear crawl; head up.
- Safety: Space kids out in lanes so nobody collides.

### Jump rope
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: None: rope-less (BBG has no skipping ropes) · 5 min
- How: BBG has no skipping ropes yet, so do it rope-less: same footwork, wrists turning an imaginary rope. Rounds of 1 min (kids) or 2-3 min (teens and adults): two-foot bounce, then alternate feet, then the boxer skip.
- Coaching points: Small jumps on the balls of the feet; wrists turn the rope, not the arms.
- Safety: Kids without the timing yet do the footwork with no rope.

### Grappling movement warm-up
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 10 min
- How: Lengths of the mat: shrimps (hip escapes), bridges, shoulder rolls forward and back, sprawls, technical stand-ups.
- Coaching points: Shoulder rolls go over the shoulder, never straight over the head and neck.
- Safety: Teach the roll from a kneeling start first.

### Breakfalls
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: Back breakfall from sitting, then squatting, then standing: tuck the chin, round the back, slap the mat with both arms. Then side breakfalls.
- Coaching points: Chin tucked the whole time; slap with the whole forearm.
- Safety: Every grappling and wrestling class until falling is automatic. Never from height.

### Shadowboxing round
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Open floor or mirror · 6 min
- How: Two 3-minute rounds: round 1 jab and footwork only, round 2 add the cross and head movement.
- Coaching points: Hands back to the face after every punch; breathe out on each shot.
- Safety: Space out so arms can't reach anyone.

## Boxing

### Stance and guard
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mirror or partner check · 6 min
- How: Feet shoulder-width, lead foot forward, rear heel up, knees soft, hands at the cheekbones, elbows in, chin down. Partners check each other.
- Coaching points: Weight on the balls of the feet. Lead toe and rear toe both point slightly in.
- Safety: None special.

### Step-drag footwork
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Tape lines or cones · 8 min
- How: Forward: lead foot steps, rear foot drags the same distance. Back, left and right the same way. Coach calls directions; athletes keep their stance.
- Coaching points: Never cross the feet or bring them together.
- Safety: Leave space between lines.

### Footwork ladder and cones
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Agility ladder or cones · 8 min
- How: In-and-out, lateral shuffle and pivot patterns through the ladder, finishing each lap with a jab-cross in the air.
- Coaching points: Quick feet, upright posture, guard stays up.
- Safety: Kids: make it a relay.

### Jab on pads
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Gloves, wraps, focus mitts · 8 min
- How: Coach holds the lead mitt. Single jabs, then double jabs, then jab-step-jab. 3 rounds of 1-2 min.
- Coaching points: Full extension, turn the fist over, snap it back to the face.
- Safety: Wraps and gloves on. Pad holder meets the punch; no swinging at kids.

### 1-2 on pads
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Gloves, wraps, focus mitts · 8 min
- How: Jab then cross. Coach calls '1' or '1-2'; add a step back after each combination.
- Coaching points: Pivot the rear foot and turn the hip on the cross; chin behind the shoulder.
- Safety: As above.

### 1-2-3 on pads
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Gloves, wraps, focus mitts · 8 min
- How: Jab, cross, lead hook. Then 1-2-3-2. Rounds of 2 min.
- Coaching points: Hook is short with the elbow at shoulder height; pivot the lead foot.
- Safety: Pad holder keeps mitts close to their own head only for adults.

### Uppercuts on pads
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Gloves, wraps, focus mitts · 6 min
- How: Coach turns the mitts palm-down. Rear uppercut, lead uppercut, then 1-2-uppercut-hook.
- Coaching points: Bend the knees and drive up; don't drop the hand before throwing.
- Safety: Short range: holder protects their face.

### Heavy bag rounds
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Heavy bags, gloves, wraps · 10 min
- How: Kids: 1-min rounds, one combination at a time. Teens and adults: 3 rounds of 2-3 min with a call-out combo each round.
- Coaching points: Move around the bag; punch, then move, then punch.
- Safety: One athlete per bag side; wraps always.

### Slip line
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: A light rope or string tied between two posts at shoulder height (no ring at BBG) · 6 min
- How: Walk the rope slipping left and right: slip outside the jab, slip inside the cross. Add a counter jab after each slip.
- Coaching points: Turn the shoulders and bend the knees a little; eyes stay forward.
- Safety: None special.

### Bob and weave under the rope
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: A light rope or string tied between two posts at chest height (no ring at BBG) · 6 min
- How: Walk the rope ducking under it in a U-shape from side to side, guard up the whole way.
- Coaching points: Bend at the knees, not the waist; come up in stance.
- Safety: Kids can do it too at a lower rope height.

### Pad defense feeds
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Focus mitts · 8 min
- How: Coach throws slow mitt jabs and hooks; athlete slips, rolls or blocks, then counters on the mitt.
- Coaching points: Start slow; the coach controls speed.
- Safety: Coach only, never partner-to-partner for beginners.

## Kids games

### Glove tag
- Ages: Kids 6-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Gloves · 8 min
- How: In pairs, try to tap the partner's gloves with your glove while they pull their hands away, staying in stance. 30-second rounds, then swap.
- Coaching points: Feet in stance, quick hands, eyes on the partner.
- Safety: Glove-to-glove taps only: no touching the head or body. Coach stops anything rough.

### Pad target game
- Ages: Kids 4-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Coloured or numbered mitts or shields · 8 min
- How: Coach holds two mitts and calls a colour or number; kids hit the right one with a jab or cross. Speed it up as they get it right.
- Coaching points: Quick hands back to the face after each shot.
- Safety: Coach holds; kids don't hold for each other until they can do it safely.

### Follow the leader footwork
- Ages: Kids 4-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Open floor · 6 min
- How: Coach (or a kid) leads with boxing steps, pivots and punches in the air; everyone copies. Switch leaders.
- Coaching points: Stance stays the same the whole time.
- Safety: None special.

## Boxing

### Coach-called combinations
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Intermediate · Contact: None · Equipment: Open floor or mirror · 6 min
- How: Shadowboxing while the coach calls numbers (1, 1-2, 1-2-3, 2-3-2). Add a defensive move after each combination.
- Coaching points: Finish every combination with the hands up and a step out.
- Safety: None special.

## Conditioning

### Bag and body-weight circuit
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Heavy bags, timer · 12 min
- How: Stations of 45 sec work / 15 sec move: bag combos, squats, bag hooks, push-ups, bag straight punches, mountain climbers. 2-3 rounds.
- Coaching points: Quality first: good stance on the bag even when tired.
- Safety: Water between rounds. Scale push-ups to knees.

## Boxing

### Body-only partner touch
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Light touch (body) · Equipment: Gloves, mouthguards · 6 min
- How: Partners in stance; light touches to the body only with the jab, then jab-cross. Coach watches every pair.
- Coaching points: Touch, don't hit. Guard up, feet moving.
- Safety: Body only, light, coach-supervised; stop if anyone speeds up. Follow the gym's youth contact rules.

### Controlled technical sparring
- Ages: Adults · Level: Advanced · Contact: Controlled sparring · Equipment: Headgear, mouthguard, 14-16 oz gloves, groin protector · 12 min
- How: Competition Class only, with Cody's OK: rounds at an agreed pace (technical, not hard), matched by size and experience, coach right beside the pair.
- Coaching points: Work the plan from class; defence first.
- Safety: Only athletes Cody approves, with a certified coach supervising. Stop at once for any sign of a head injury and follow the Rowan's Law removal steps. MMA: no head strikes for anyone under 19.

## Kickboxing

### Teep on the shield
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Kick shields · 8 min
- How: Lead-leg push kick into a held shield, then rear-leg. Add a step-in teep.
- Coaching points: Knee up first, push with the ball of the foot, hips forward, return to stance.
- Safety: Holder braces with the shield against the body, not the arms.

### Roundhouse to the body shield
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Beginner · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Kick shields · 8 min
- How: Rear-leg roundhouse into a shield held at the ribs. 10 each side, then off a jab.
- Coaching points: Pivot on the ball of the base foot, turn the hip over, strike with the shin.
- Safety: Holder stands side-on and braces.

### Switch kick
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Kick shields or Thai pads · 6 min
- How: Quick switch of the feet, then a lead-side roundhouse to the shield.
- Coaching points: Small switch, hands up during the switch.
- Safety: As above.

### Jab-cross-kick on pads
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Thai pads or shield · 8 min
- How: 1-2 then rear roundhouse to the body; then 1-2-lead hook-rear kick.
- Coaching points: The cross loads the hip for the kick.
- Safety: Holder calls the combination and controls the pace.

### Low-kick checks (slow)
- Ages: Teens & adults · Level: Intermediate · Contact: None · Equipment: None (no contact: the kick stops short) · 6 min
- How: Partner throws a slow low kick that stops short of the thigh; the other lifts the shin to check. Swap every 10.
- Coaching points: Knee up and turned out; hands stay up.
- Safety: No contact: the kick stops short. Shin-to-shin contact only with shin guards (BBG doesn't have them) and Cody's OK.

## Wrestling

### Stance and motion
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: Wrestling stance (knees bent, back straight, head up, hands in front). Move forward, back, circle, and sprawl on the whistle.
- Coaching points: Head up, hips under the shoulders, short steps.
- Safety: None special.

### Penetration step
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: Level change, step deep with the lead foot, drop the lead knee to the mat, bring the back foot up. Kids start from the knees.
- Coaching points: Back straight, head up; the knee goes down softly.
- Safety: Mats only. Knee pads if anyone has sore knees.

### Sprawl drill
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 5 min
- How: From stance, shoot the legs back and drop the hips to the mat on the whistle, then back to stance. 10-20 reps.
- Coaching points: Hips heavy, hands down in front, head up.
- Safety: Land on the hips, not the knees.

### Hand fighting
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: In pairs: inside tie (hand on the biceps), wrist control, inside control. 30-second rounds trying to win the hand position.
- Coaching points: Control the wrists, keep the elbows in.
- Safety: No grabbing the head or neck, no slaps.

### Double-leg takedown (drill)
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 10 min
- How: Penetration step into the hips, hands behind the knees, drive and turn the corner. Partner breakfalls. 5 each, slow, then faster.
- Coaching points: Head on the outside, drive through, don't lift.
- Safety: Partner who falls must know breakfalls. No lifting and slamming.

### Single-leg and finish
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 10 min
- How: Shoot to a single leg, head inside, then finish by running the pipe (turning the corner).
- Coaching points: Head up, hips in, keep the leg tight to the chest.
- Safety: Finish to the mat under control; no slams.

### Stand-up from bottom
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From referee's position on the bottom: base, stand up, hand control, turn to face.
- Coaching points: Back to the partner's chest, control the hands, then turn.
- Safety: Top partner gives light resistance.

### Half nelson turn
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From the top: breakdown, then a half nelson on the far arm to turn the opponent to the back for a pin.
- Coaching points: Pressure goes through the shoulder, not the head or neck.
- Safety: A half nelson is legal; full and three-quarter nelsons are banned for kids. Never crank the neck; no forcing.

## Kids games

### Knee tag
- Ages: Kids 6-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: In pairs, in wrestling stance, try to tag the partner's knees while protecting your own. 30-second rounds.
- Coaching points: Stay in stance, move the feet, hands down to defend.
- Safety: Tags only; no grabbing.

### Sumo push
- Ages: Kids 6-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats, a marked circle · 6 min
- How: Partners push chest-to-chest or hand-to-shoulder to move each other out of a circle. No throws.
- Coaching points: Low stance, drive with the legs.
- Safety: Match kids by size. Stop when one steps out.

## Wrestling

### Live wrestling from the knees
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Live grappling · Equipment: Mats · 10 min
- How: Start on the knees facing each other; first to get behind wins the point. 30-60-second goes, matched by size.
- Coaching points: Use the moves from class.
- Safety: Coach matches by size and age, watches every pair, and stops for unsafe holds.

## Jiu-jitsu

### Shrimp chains
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 5 min
- How: Lengths of the mat hip-escaping, both sides.
- Coaching points: Push off the foot, move the hips away, frame with the arms.
- Safety: None special.

### Technical stand-up
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 5 min
- How: From sitting: post a hand behind, the opposite foot in front, lift the hips and swing the leg back to stand in base.
- Coaching points: Front hand protects the face the whole time.
- Safety: None special.

### Tap practice
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 3 min
- How: Practise tapping: hand on the partner or the mat, three times, and say 'tap'. Partner lets go at once.
- Coaching points: Tap early. Letting go at once is as important as tapping.
- Safety: Every grappling class with kids or beginners.

### Mount escape: bridge and roll
- Ages: Ages 6+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: Trap an arm and the same-side foot, bridge high over the shoulder and roll to the top.
- Coaching points: Bridge onto the shoulder, not straight up.
- Safety: Top partner lets themselves be rolled at first.

### Mount escape: elbow-knee
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: Frame on the hip, shrimp, and slide the knee under to recover half guard, then full guard.
- Coaching points: Elbows tight; small shrimps.
- Safety: Light resistance only.

### Guard retention frames
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: Partner walks around the guard; bottom player frames on the shoulder and hip, hip-escapes and keeps the knees between.
- Coaching points: Feet on the hips, knees in, keep turning to face.
- Safety: Slow first.

### Scissor sweep
- Ages: Youth 11-14 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From closed guard: control a wrist and the opposite elbow, shin across the belly, chop the near leg and sweep to mount.
- Coaching points: Pull the partner forward first, then scissor.
- Safety: Partner falls to the side under control.

### Toreando pass
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: Grip the pants or knees, push the legs to one side and step around to side control.
- Coaching points: Move the hips around fast; chest down once past.
- Safety: Light resistance only.

### Armbar from mount
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From mount: isolate an arm, step around the head, fall back with the knees pinched and the thumb up. Apply slowly.
- Coaching points: Hips under the elbow; apply slowly and stop at the tap.
- Safety: Slow pressure, tap rule enforced. Ontario's youth grappling rules allow the straight armbar from age 7; BBG teaches it in Jiu-Jitsu (ages 12+).

### Rear naked choke from the back
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From back control with hooks: arm across the neck, hand to the bicep, other hand behind the head, squeeze slowly.
- Coaching points: Get the elbow under the chin; squeeze slowly.
- Safety: Slow pressure, let go at the tap or if the partner goes limp. Ontario's youth grappling rules allow it from age 11; BBG teaches it in Jiu-Jitsu (ages 12+).

### Positional sparring: guard vs pass
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Live grappling · Equipment: Mats · 12 min
- How: One partner starts in guard. Top player scores by passing; bottom scores by sweeping or standing up. Reset and swap.
- Coaching points: Use the moves from class.
- Safety: Coach matches by size and experience; watch every pair.

## Kids games

### King of the mat
- Ages: Kids 6-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Live grappling · Equipment: Mats · 8 min
- How: From the knees: one kid holds the mat, challengers try to get them on their back or off the mat; winner stays.
- Coaching points: Stay on the knees; use pins, not pulling.
- Safety: Match by size; no pulling the head, no submissions.

### Turtle flip
- Ages: Kids 6-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: Live grappling · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: One kid in turtle (on knees and elbows); the partner tries to roll them onto the back in 30 seconds.
- Coaching points: Bottom keeps the elbows tight; top works around.
- Safety: No grabbing the head or neck.

## MMA

### Sprawl and strike shadow
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Open floor · 5 min
- How: Shadowbox; on the whistle sprawl, get back up and throw a 1-2.
- Coaching points: Back up to stance quickly with the hands up.
- Safety: None special.

### Strike to level change on pads
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Focus mitts, mats · 8 min
- How: 1-2 on the mitts, then level change and penetration step to the coach's hips (coach blocks with the shield).
- Coaching points: Punches set up the shot; head up on the level change.
- Safety: Coach braces; no full takedown on this drill.

### Pummeling for underhooks
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats · 6 min
- How: In the clinch, swim the arms inside for underhooks, alternating at an easy pace, then compete for 30 seconds.
- Coaching points: Head on the same side as the underhook; hips close.
- Safety: No slamming.

### Wall walk get-up
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Partner drilling · Equipment: Mats against a wall (no cage at BBG) · 8 min
- How: From sitting against the padded wall: underhook, post a hand, walk the back up the wall to stand, and turn out.
- Coaching points: Keep the head up and the hips close to the wall.
- Safety: Padded wall only.

### Ground strikes on the bag
- Ages: Adults · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Pads/bag · Equipment: Heavy bag on the floor, gloves · 6 min
- How: Bag on the mat: from the top position throw controlled punches to the bag, posture up, base wide.
- Coaching points: Posture before punching; hips stay heavy.
- Safety: Bag only, never a partner in class.

### Clinch positional sparring
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Intermediate · Contact: Live grappling · Equipment: Mats · 10 min
- How: Start in the clinch: score by taking the back, a takedown or separating to stand. No strikes.
- Coaching points: Win the underhooks first.
- Safety: No strikes; coach watches every pair.

## Conditioning

### Body-weight intervals
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Timer · 8 min
- How: 8 rounds of 20 sec work, 10 sec rest: squats, push-ups, mountain climbers, burpees (repeat).
- Coaching points: Move well before moving fast.
- Safety: Scale every movement; water after.

### Battle rope intervals
- Ages: Ages 12+ · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Battle ropes, timer · 8 min
- How: Rounds of 20 sec on, 40 sec off: two-hand waves, alternating waves, then slams. 4-6 rounds. The partner waiting does squats or holds a plank, then swap.
- Coaching points: Hips back, knees soft, core braced; the power comes from the legs, not just the arms.
- Safety: Anchor the rope securely and keep a clear lane on both sides. Stop if the lower back hurts.

## Kids games

### Relay races
- Ages: Kids 4-10 · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Cones · 8 min
- How: Teams race bear crawls, crab walks and sprints to a cone and back.
- Coaching points: Cheer the team; good form counts.
- Safety: Lanes well apart.

## Cool-down

### Cool-down stretch and breathing
- Ages: All ages · Level: Beginner · Contact: None · Equipment: Mats · 5 min
- How: Easy stretches for the hips, hamstrings, shoulders and neck, then 5 slow breaths in through the nose and out through the mouth.
- Coaching points: Gentle; no bouncing.
- Safety: Ask how everyone feels before they leave.
