## Equipment at BBG (Cody, October 2026)

Plans only use this gear: Heavy bags, Focus mitts, Thai pads and kick shields, Wrestling mats, Gloves to lend, Headgear, Agility ladders and cones, Medicine balls, Battle ropes.

Not at BBG: boxing ring, cage, skipping ropes. Slip and bob-and-weave lines use a light rope or string tied between two posts; jump rope is done rope-less.
