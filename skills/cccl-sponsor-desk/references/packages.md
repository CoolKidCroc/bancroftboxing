# CCCL sponsor packages

These are Cody's 2026 packages. He's using them again for 2027 unless he changes them. Cody calls Title "Platinum". He listed Silver at $2,500 in October 2026; quote $2,000 (the 2026 price) until Cody confirms. The Packages tab on the Pipeline sheet is the source of truth; use this file only if the sheet isn't available, and say so.

| Package | Price | What the sponsor gets | Suggested slots per show |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title Sponsor | $7,500 | Exclusive presenting sponsorship and naming rights ("CCCL presented by…"). Premier logo on the MMA cage and event banners. Featured on posters, advertising and digital marketing. Recognised during fighter introductions and announcements. Dedicated social media posts before and after the event. Premium VIP seating for their people or guests. Space to display promotional material and meet spectators. Recognised as the event's leading corporate partner. Biggest logo on the event shirt and "presented by" on the livestream. | 1 |
| Gold Sponsor | $4,000 | Priority logo placement around the competition area. Featured on event posters and advertising. Social media promotion and sponsor appreciation posts. Several live announcements during the event. VIP tickets. Space for their banners and promotional material. Recognition across event marketing. Large logo on the event shirt and the livestream. | 2 |
| Silver Sponsor | $2,000 | Logo on selected promotional materials. Recognition on CCCL social media. Shared banner or advertising space at the venue. Live recognition from the announcer. Free event tickets. Recognised as an official event sponsor. Logo on the event shirt and the livestream. | 4 |
| Bronze Sponsor | $1,000 | Logo on selected event advertising. Social media sponsor announcement. Name in the event sponsor thank-yous. Live recognition during the event. Free admission tickets. Small logo on the event shirt and the livestream sponsor slide. | 10 |
| Community Sponsor | $300–$500 | Recognition on CCCL social media. Name or logo in selected sponsor promotions. Recognised as an official community supporter. Name on the event shirt and the livestream sponsor slide. | 20 |
| VIP Corporate Table | $1,500 | Cage-side table for 8-10 guests (depends on the venue's tables) for a business to host staff or clients. | 6 |

Notes:
- **Bronze and Community don't include their own banner.** Banners start at Silver (shared space). Don't promise a banner to a $1,000 sponsor.
- **VIP table**: $1,500. It seats 8-10 depending on the venue's tables, so say "8-10 guests" unless the venue is booked and you know the table size. 6 were sold in 2026.
- Booths, event partnerships and custom packages can be discussed one-on-one. Anything custom needs Cody's OK before it goes in writing.
- "Suggested slots" are a suggestion, not a promise to sponsors. Title is always one per show.
- **Livestream and event shirt**: every level gets its logo or name on both, sized by level (Kyle, Oct 2026). Cody confirmed the sizes above (Oct 20, 2026): biggest for Title, down to name only for Community.
