# CCCL Canva starter designs

16 designs in **CCCL / Marketing / Canva starter designs**. Each is a PNG (to look at) and a PDF (to import into Canva: Create a design > Import file). The background (dark gradient, pines, red glow) is one image: lock it in Canva. Every word is its own text box. The sample text (CCCL 2, Haliburton, Saturday May 1 2027, A.J. LaRue Arena, "Fighter name") is there to be replaced.

When Cody asks for a graphic, name the design by number and give the exact text for every box below, in order. Use only confirmed facts: names from the Bout Card and Fighters tabs (adults who agreed), prices and times from Show Setup, official results only.
