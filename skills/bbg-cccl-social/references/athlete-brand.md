# Cody's athlete brand (his own Facebook and Instagram)

From The Constrictor Athlete Brand Plan (Oct 2026). Cody's answers: fight career, training and technique, coaching, speaking; Facebook and Instagram; goals: grow the gym, sell CCCL tickets, personal sponsors, merch, a following; 1 to 3 hours a week.

**Theme: Get back up.** Cody's own words (Bancroft This Week, 2019): "If you have a dream, fight for it! No matter how many times you get knocked down. Get back up and keep going."

- **His words**: "If you have a dream, fight for it! No matter how many times you get knocked down. Get back up and keep going." (Bancroft This Week, Jan 2019)  "We're not just running a gym here; we're building a platform for local talent." (Bancroft This Week, Feb 2026)
- **The gym**: Opened Bancroft Boxing & Grappling in 2025; about 90 members by March 2026. Cody coaches every class. (The Highlander; Cody)
- **CCCL**: Promoter of the Cottage Country Combat League: first show May 2, 2026, A.J. LaRue Arena, Haliburton; 400 tickets sold. (Cody; Sherdog event page)

## The four kinds of posts

- **Constrictor Tips** (Mondays): One move in under a minute: submissions, wrestling, boxing basics. Shows he knows his stuff and brings people to the gym.
- **Behind the scenes** (Wednesdays): A real day: coaching, booking venues, building a show. Shows the work behind BBG and CCCL.
- **Get back up** (Fridays): His story and what fighting taught him, in his own words. The heart of the brand, and the bridge to Second Chance later.
- **Shows and community** (As needed): CCCL countdowns, fighter shout-outs, appearances. Swapped in during show weeks.

## Weekly routine

- **Once a week, 60 min**: Film in one go at the gym: 3-4 Constrictor Tips (one take each, phone on a tripod, vertical) and 2 short behind-the-scenes clips. Adults only on camera unless a parent has said yes.
- **Once a week, 20 min**: Send Claude the clips' topics or a voice note. Claude drafts the week's captions in Cody's voice; Cody changes anything that doesn't sound like him.
- **Once a week, 10 min**: Schedule the week in Meta Business Suite (free; posts to Facebook and Instagram at once).
- **Most days, 5 min**: Reply to comments and messages. Gym questions get the free-first-class link; anything about a child goes to a parent conversation.

## Rules for his page

- Real facts only. His record is public (1-5 on Sherdog): he can talk about it honestly or not at all, but nothing gets rounded up.
- Kids: never on Cody's page unless a parent has agreed; first names only. No youth in sponsor posts.
- Results: only official ones. No trash talk; respect every fighter.
- Health and injuries: only Cody's own, and only if he wants to share.
- Sponsors on his page follow the CCCL rules: no cannabis or vape, alcohol and betting flagged to Cody first, and CCCL's sponsors get first chance before their competitors.
- His personal email and phone stay off his page; business goes to ontario.cccl@gmail.com or the gym.
