# CCCL 2027 venues (researched October 2026)

Cody's rules for 2027 (Oct 2026): 4+ shows, up to 3 hours from Bancroft, crowds of 1,000 or more, months Jan, Feb, Apr, May, Jun, Aug, Nov. Venue types he's open to: hockey arena, community hall, fairgrounds (summer), casino or event centre.

Scores 1-5 (5 = best): crowd (chance of 1,000+), venue, fighters (gyms nearby), competition (5 = little), distance (5 under 1 h, 4 1-2 h, 3 2-2.5 h, 2 2.5-3 h, 1 over 3 h). Overall = simple average. Drive times are Rome2rio estimates. Capacities and rates are quoted as published; re-check before Cody relies on them.

## Key points

- **Winter (Jan, Feb, Nov)**: small-town arenas have ice. Venues with evidence of winter floor events: Peterborough Memorial Centre, Slush Puppie Place (Kingston), Tribute Communities Centre and the Children's Arena (Oshawa), Pickering Casino Resort arena, Casino Rama.
- **1,000+ crowd**: show 1 sold about 400 tickets with 400-600 people in the building. No small-town venue is proven at 1,000. Marmora's arena is the only small venue that publishes a 1,000-person dry floor ($600/day, May-Aug).
- **Over 3 hours**: Niagara Falls (~4 h 29 min) and Alliston (~3 h 16 min). Not for 2027.
- **Lead time**: OAC event permit at least 30 days before the show; rule-change exemptions 120 days before. January 2027 is very tight.
- **Unknowns**: no big venue publishes rental costs; small-town floor capacities aren't published; 2027 OHL home schedules decide winter dates; casinos may not rent to an amateur promotion.
