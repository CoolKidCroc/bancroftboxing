## Bancroft NHCC status
Renovation essentially complete and ice back in as of mid/late Sept 2026. The Town said on Sept 15, 2026 that the remaining Green and Inclusive Community Buildings work was done (fully accessible washroom, new referee change room, extra small change room) and that ice would be available the following Monday (~Sept 21, 2026), running on a temporary ice plant until a small US-made part for the new permanent plant arrived (expected end of September), with the changeover taking about a month and not expected to disrupt bookings. Project scope: replace the original ice-making equipment, new accessible washroom, new heat pumps; budget just over $2.4M ($1.9M federal GICB grant, just under $500k Town). The new cooling plant arrived at the end of August instead of July, so minor hockey used Wilberforce's arena in early September. Note: the Town's NHCC page text says public skating 'has come to an end for the 2026-2027 season', which looks like a stale/mislabelled line.
Timeline: Jan 13, 2026: mayor says GICB funding secured in 2025 and Town preparing for the 2026 arena retrofit. Mar 9-10, 2026: tenders to council (~$2M project, ~$500k Town share); work to start soon because ice usually goes in in August. Late July 2026: Town learns of equipment delay. End of Aug 2026: cooling plant delivered. ~Sept 21, 2026: ice available (temporary plant). ~Oct 2026: switch to permanent plant (about one month). For 2027 shows: the arena should be fully operational; a spring/early-summer 2027 dry-floor date is the realistic window (estimate) - the ice normally goes in in August and the Rockhound Gemboree uses NHCC over the Civic Holiday weekend.

## Ontario Athletics Commission (amateur events)
**Update (Oct 17, 2026):** newer research on O. Reg. 507/24 says one $600 permit should cover a whole card (a "contest" is one bout or several consecutive bouts) and the $3,000 permit covers up to 8 events in a year. Weigh-ins are on show day within 3 hours of the first bout. See the CCCL Fight Night Kit Rules tab.
Amateur MMA has been legal in Ontario under the Combative Sports Act, 2019 since Jan 1, 2025; all amateur MMA contests must be sanctioned by the Ontario Athletics Commission (OAC). Amateur MMA ruleset v2.0 took effect May 27, 2025. EVENT PERMIT (ontario.ca 'Amateur combative sports events', updated Sept 16, 2025): apply at least 30 days before the event (for a series, 30 days before the first and details 30 days before each later one). Non-refundable application fees: "$600 for an event permit to hold a single amateur combative sport contest or exhibition"; "$1,600 for ... a tournament-style" event; "$3,000 for an event permit to hold up to eight single amateur combative sport contests or exhibitions". No published tier above eight contests - with 8-12 bouts (CCCL 1), ask the OAC whether a second permit or another option applies (uncertainty). Required with application: application form, photo ID, partnership/incorporation proof, code of conduct, venue letter of undertaking confirming the date, policies for qualification and selection of officials, list of all onsite medical personnel with titles/contacts, emergency response policies, certificate of commercial general liability insurance with minimum coverage of $2,000,000. Medical: if full-contact strikes to the head are allowed, a physician licensed with the College of Physicians and Surgeons of Ontario must be present at all times; each contestant needs a medical exam by a legally qualified practitioner within one year before the event. Rules: modified-ruleset exemptions must be requested at least 120 days before the event (AthleticsComm@ontario.ca). After: keep amateur books of suspensions, injuries, results; post-event report due within 14 days. Refusals can be appealed to the Licence Appeals Tribunal. Secondary source (MM-Eh, Jan 2025): promoters, contestants, managers, matchmakers and officials must be licensed, and amateurs must complete concussion-awareness training and follow return-to-play protocols - confirm which licences apply to amateur shows. OAC random anti-doping testing from Jan 1, 2026 applies to professionals, not amateurs (MM-Eh, Jan 2026). OAC contact (from ontario.ca pro page): AthleticsComm@ontario.ca, 416-326-0416. Venue-side insurance seen: Minden and North Kawartha require $5M liability for liquor events; Highlands East requires $2M for all events.
Sources: https://www.ontario.ca/page/amateur-combative-sports; https://www.ontario.ca/document/amateur-combative-sport-rules-mixed-martial-arts; https://ontario.ca/page/combat-sports-regulation-and-fees; https://mm-eh.ca/2025/01/ontario-welcomes-amateur-mma-as-new-legislation-comes-into-force; https://mm-eh.ca/tag/ontario/
