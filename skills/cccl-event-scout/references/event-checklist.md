# CCCL show checklist (amateur MMA, Ontario)

A working list built from the first show, not legal advice. The full fight-night version, with due dates, is the Countdown tab of the CCCL Fight Night Kit (and the cccl-fight-night skill). Requirements come from the Ontario Athletics Commission (OAC) under the Combative Sports Act, 2019; confirm every item with the OAC and the insurer for each show.

## 6+ months out
- [ ] Pick town and date (check clashes: other combat shows within ~2 h, fairs, festivals, long weekends, big UFC cards)
- [ ] Venue quote: rental rate for setup day + show day, capacity, chairs/tables, staff, bar, load-in for the cage
- [ ] Run the Event Profit Predictor with the real quote; decide go / no-go
- [ ] Municipal or council approval if the venue requires it (Dysart et al council approved show 1)
- [ ] Hold the date with a deposit

## 4-6 months out
- [ ] Apply for the OAC amateur event permit at least 30 days ahead (120 days if asking for any change to the standard ruleset). Fees: $600 for one show, $1,600 tournament-style, $3,000 for up to 8 shows in a year. Under O. Reg. 507/24 a "contest" is one bout or several consecutive bouts, so one $600 permit should cover the whole card: confirm with the OAC. The application needs: form, photo ID, proof of partnership/incorporation, code of conduct, a letter from the venue confirming the date, the officials' selection policy, the list of on-site medical staff, emergency response policies and a $2M commercial general liability insurance certificate.
- [ ] No OAC licences for an amateur show: they're only for professional promoters, fighters and officials. CCCL needs the event permit above (see the Fight Night Kit's Rules tab)
- [ ] Book officials for every bout (referee, 3 judges, timekeeper, scorekeeper) and confirm the OAC accepts CCCL's officials list when you apply for the permit
- [ ] Book medical: an Ontario-licensed physician on site the whole time plus at least one more medical person. An ambulance is a professional-permit rule; ask the OAC if it wants one
- [ ] Event insurance quote and certificate for the venue
- [ ] Cage rental and delivery booked: the cage must sit on a platform raised 3-4 ft
- [ ] Sponsorship push starts (Sponsorship Pipeline)
- [ ] Tickets on sale (Eventbrite): GA, VIP seat, VIP table

## 2-3 months out
- [ ] Fighter recruitment from Ontario gyms; collect applications, records, weights
- [ ] Security company booked
- [ ] Lighting, sound, staging and livestream crew booked
- [ ] Poster with sponsor logos; social countdown starts
- [ ] Volunteers and staff list

## Fight month
- [ ] Bout card set by Cody and officials; submit to the OAC as required
- [ ] Each fighter's medical exam (within the past year) and paperwork as the OAC requires (never stored in the shared sheets)
- [ ] Banners printed; sponsor deliverables checked (Fulfilment tab)
- [ ] Run of show: weigh-ins on show day within 3 hours of the first bout, doors, walkouts, intermissions, sponsor shout-outs
- [ ] Ticket sales vs break-even checked weekly

## After the show
- [ ] Sponsor thank-yous with photos within 3 days
- [ ] Real numbers into the Predictor (Haliburton 2026 Check tab or a new column)
- [ ] Post-event report to the OAC within 14 days
- [ ] Results report: tickets, crowd, sponsors, costs, profit, what to change
