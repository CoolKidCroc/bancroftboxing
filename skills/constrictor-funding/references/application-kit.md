## Tips from the funders

- Canada Summer Jobs 2027 opens Oct 27, 2026 at 9:00 am ET. Have the CRA business number and payroll deductions account ready. Jobs must be 30-40 hours/week for 6-16 consecutive weeks. Private employers get up to 50% of minimum wage with no MERCs; not-for-profits get up to 100% plus MERCs. Write the application to the riding's local priorities (2026 priorities for Hastings-Lennox and Addington-Tyendinaga included community events and local/regional tourism development). (canada.ca CSJ applicant guide; CFIB CSJ page; CSJ local priorities page)
- KidSport only pays for clubs that are members of their Provincial Sport Organization. Boxing Ontario club registration needs at least one NCCP Level 1 Instruction Beginner coach, a safety inspection, and staff registered with Boxing Ontario; registration runs to Dec 31 each year. BBG isn't registered (Cody, Oct 20, 2026). Decide whether to register: it's the only way families could use KidSport at BBG (Dec 4, 2026 deadline for Jan-Feb 2027 programs). (kidsportcanada.ca/ontario/provincial-fund; boxingontario.com becoming a registered club)
- Structure kids' programs as sessions of at least 5 weeks (one or more classes per week) or camps of at least 5 consecutive days so they qualify for Jumpstart individual grants. BBG is already a Jumpstart payee (Cody, Oct 2026); cheques go to the provider, not the family. Families should apply before the program starts. (jumpstart.canadiantire.ca individual-child-grants and FAQ)
- OTF requires nonprofits to have 12 months of incorporation, operating and financial history, at least 3 board members, and Board-approved financial statements. Incorporate Second Chance as early as possible so the 12-month clock starts. Seed applications are scored Strategy 50%, Process 25%, People 25%. (otf.ca eligibility policy and Seed grant page)
- Several funders (MLSE Foundation, RBC, TELUS, United Way, 100 Women Who Care) require qualified-donee status: a registered charity, or a registered Canadian amateur athletic association for sport. MLSE lets non-qualified groups apply through a qualified-donee trustee. Partnering with an existing local charity as lead or trustee is the fastest route for youth or employment projects. (mlsefoundation.org community grants; rbc.com apply for funding; telus.com community grants; unitedwayhpe.ca/funding)
- Jumpstart Community Development Grants recommend booking a call with your regional Relationship Manager before applying. Its Youth Development stream (up to $75,000) funds sport programs where coaching, mentorship or employment is core, which matches a Second Chance + BBG youth employment program. Next intake opens Mar 10, 2027. (jumpstart.canadiantire.ca/pages/community-development-grants)
- Rural Ontario Development business funding is first-come, first-served, and each business gets only one successful application. Costs incurred before the project's effective date are ineligible. Fill the form in Adobe Acrobat (not a browser), email it to RODProgram@ontario.ca, and register on Transfer Payment Ontario first. The next window is Jan 18 to Feb 25, 2027. (ontario.ca ROD page and business development application form)
- Starter Company Plus requires a cash or in-kind contribution of at least 25% of the grant. Hastings County businesses apply through the Small Business Centre in Belleville (2026 intake was Apr 13 to May 22), so plan a business plan and cash flow for a spring 2027 intake. (ontario.ca/page/starter-company-plus; smallbusinessctr.com/services)
- CFDC loan applications need the amount, its purpose and how it helps the business, a meeting with staff, the Loan Application (or Ready Money form under $10,000), and a cash flow projection. Owners aged 18-39 can get the Young Entrepreneur Loan with no payments in year one. (community-futures.ca business loans)
- Ontario Job Grant: employers with under 100 employees pay at least one-sixth of training costs. Training must be from an eligible third-party provider with 2+ years of experience delivering it, and the employer must have operated in Ontario for at least a year. Up to $15,000 with no employer share is possible when training an unemployed new hire, which could pair well with Second Chance clients hired by partner employers. (ontario.ca/page/ontario-job-grant)
- The Coaches Association of Ontario Community Coaching Grant lets individual coaches apply (up to $1,000 for mats, protective gear or safety training). Each BBG coach could apply separately when it reopens (2026 window closed June 5). Purchases made before acceptance are not reimbursed. (safesport101.coachesontario.ca/grant)
- The Community Sport and Recreation Infrastructure Fund caps for-profit applicants at 25% provincial cost share, requires audited financial statements and a corporate structure document, and closes Dec 22, 2026. Only pursue it if a new or expanded BBG facility is planned. (ontario.ca CSRIF Stream 2)
- The County of Haliburton is hosting a Funders Forum on November 5, 2026 for local businesses, nonprofits and community groups, a chance to meet funders relevant to CCCL's Haliburton shows. (haliburtoncounty.ca home page)
- United Way Hastings & Prince Edward: applications open Oct 23, 2026, training session Nov 2, due Dec 11, 2026 through e-CIMPACT. Minimum request is $5,000, and audited or reviewed statements plus a volunteer board are required, so this is a goal for Second Chance after it becomes a charity. (unitedwayhpe.ca/funding)
- Track where CCCL ticket buyers come from (postal codes). Tourism funders define a visitor or tourist as someone travelling 40+ km (Experience Ontario, RTO8), and this data is what tourism partners and municipalities will ask for. (EO26 guidelines; rto8.com partnership program)
- MLSE Foundation Community Action Grant: expression of interest opens Nov 3, 2026, info webinar Nov 17, closes Dec 15, 2026. Women- and girl-identified youth programming is a primary focus, which suits BBG's women's boxing if delivered with a qualified-donee lead. (mlsefoundation.org)
