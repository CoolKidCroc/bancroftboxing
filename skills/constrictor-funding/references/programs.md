### Canada Small Business Financing Program (CSBFP) (Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (delivered by banks and credit unions))
- For: BBG, CCCL · Who can apply: Small businesses or start-ups operating in Canada with gross annual revenues of $10 million or less (farming excluded)
- Funds: Government-backed loans for equipment, leasehold improvements, real property, intangible assets and working capital; lines of credit · Amount: Up to $1.15M per borrower: term loans up to $1M (max $500K for leasehold improvements and equipment, of which up to $150K for intangibles and working capital); lines of credit up to $150K

- Source (high confidence): https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/canada-small-business-financing-program/en/find-loan-your-small-business/about-program/helping-small-businesses-get-loans
- Notes: Cody is thinking about a new or bigger space (Cody, Oct 20, 2026): CSBFP loans can cover leasehold improvements and equipment as well as gear. Good route for gym equipment (mats, ring, bags) or a cage/ring and production gear for CCCL shows.
