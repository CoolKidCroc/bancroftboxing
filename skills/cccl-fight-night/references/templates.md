**Messages, forms, scripts and promo copy for running and selling a show. Fill in the [brackets], read it once, send.**

**House rules:** Cody sends everything himself. Contact fighters through their gym or coach. Never ask for or write down medical details: the physician and the Athletics Commission handle those. Youth athletes: talk to the parent, first name and last initial only, no photos without a parent's OK. No prize worth \$300 or more (it would make a fighter a pro). Results only once they're official. Check every rule against the Rules tab of the Fight Night Kit and the Commission.

**3. Fighter sign-up form (make it in Google Forms)**

*Takes about 10 minutes. In Google Forms (signed in as ontario.cccl@gmail.com), make a new form called 'CCCL [Town] fighter sign-up', add these questions, and link the responses to a Google Sheet. Copy accepted fighters onto the Fighters tab.*

**Adult amateur MMA**

- Full name, as it should appear on the card (short answer, required)

- Nickname, if any (short answer)

- Gym or team (short answer, required)

- Head coach's name and the gym's email (short answer, required)

- Fight weight you'll make (lb) (short answer, required)

- Amateur MMA record: wins and losses, and a link to it if it's online (short answer, required)

- Other amateur contests in any combat sport (boxing, kickboxing, wrestling...): how many and where (short answer, required). This sets the experience class: Pre-Novice 0-5, Novice 6-10, Open 11+.

- Age division: Senior (19+), Youth A (16-18), Youth B (14-15), Youth C (12-13) (multiple choice, required). Under 18: a parent or guardian must also sign.

- Any professional fights, or ever paid a purse or prize worth \$300 or more? (Yes / No, required). If yes, they may not count as an amateur: check with the Commission.

- Which show dates can you do? (checkboxes)

- Walkout song (short answer)

- Two lines about you for the announcer: hometown, job, why you fight (paragraph)

- OK to use your name, photo and video to promote the show? (Yes / No, required)

- I understand I'll need a medical exam and Ontario Athletics Commission paperwork, and that medical information goes to the Commission and the physician, not to CCCL (checkbox, required)

**Don't add questions about injuries, medical history or health.** That information goes through the Commission's process, not the promoter's form.

**Youth grappling (filled in by a parent or guardian)**

- Athlete's first name and last initial (required)

- Age group (required): use the grappling ruleset's age divisions (see the Rules tab of the Fight Night Kit)

- Weight (lb) and club (required)

- Years of training: under 1, 1-3, 3 or more (required)

- I've reviewed the concussion awareness resources with my child (Rowan's Law) (checkbox, required)

- Parent or guardian's name and email (required)

- I give permission for my child to compete in youth grappling at CCCL [Town] on [date] (checkbox, required)

- OK to show my child in photos or video? (Yes / No, required)

**7. Announcer script**

*Print it for the announcer with the bout card. Sponsor reads follow each sponsor's level: check the Packages and Fulfilment tabs.*

> **Opening (about 5 minutes before bout 1)**
>
> "Good evening [Town]! Welcome to the Cottage Country Combat League, presented by [Title Sponsor]! Tonight: [number] bouts of amateur mixed martial arts, sanctioned by the Ontario Athletics Commission."
>
> "A few things: stay in your seats during the bouts, keep the aisles clear, and cheer loud. Washrooms are [where]. [The licensed area is 19+ only.]"
>
> **Sponsor reads by level**
>
> Title: at the opening, before the main event and at the close. "[Business], your Title Sponsor: [one line about them]."
>
> Gold: two reads during the night. "This bout is brought to you by [Business]."
>
> Silver: one read. "Thanks to [Business] for supporting tonight's show."
>
> Bronze: grouped read at intermission. "Big thanks to our Bronze sponsors: [names]."
>
> Community: grouped thank-you at the close. "And thank you to our community supporters: [names]."
>
> **Fighter introductions**
>
> "Fighting out of the blue corner, from [gym] in [town], with a record of [W-L], weighing in at [weight] pounds: [name]!" Then the red corner the same way.
>
> **Results (only what the officials hand you)**
>
> "Ladies and gentlemen, we go to the judges' scorecards..." or "Your winner, by [method] at [time] of round [number]: [name]!"
>
> **Intermission**
>
> "We'll take a [number]-minute break. Grab a drink, visit the merch table, and thank our sponsors: [Bronze names]."
>
> **Close**
>
> "Thank you [Town]! Thanks to our fighters, coaches, officials, volunteers and [Title Sponsor]. Our next show is [Town] on [date]. Get home safe!"
